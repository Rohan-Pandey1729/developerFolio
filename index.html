About

I'm a Research Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer with a passion for pushing the boundaries of technology and science. I'm pursuing Applied and Computational Mathematical Sciences (Scientific Computing & Numerical Analysis) at the University of Washington.

I'm also minoring in Neural Computation and Engineering, focusing on AI in neuroscience. My work spans reinforcement learning for polynomial synthesis (GNNs, PPO), cancer modeling for CAR T-cell therapy, and deep learning / adversarial ML for robust systems.

When I'm not in the lab or building rockets, I'm on the tennis court at UW or winning at Monopoly. I love collaborating and exploring new tech.
